# Try Tollgate in 3 minutes

Tollgate lets creators get paid per use of their work — no wallet, no crypto knowledge needed. Two ways to try it. Pick either; both take a couple of minutes.

## Option A — List a photo and get a pay-gated page (Aperture)

1. Go to **https://tollgate.gudman.xyz/aperture/login**
2. Type your **email** and hit **Send login link**. Check your inbox (peek at the *Updates/Promotions* tab or search `from:no-reply@send.gudman.xyz` if it's not in your main inbox) and click the link. That's it — you now have an account and a wallet was created for you automatically.
3. Click **Sell your photos** → choose **Upload a file** (or paste an image link), add a **title** and a short **description**, and submit.
4. You'll get a **watermarked preview** and a shareable link. Open **Browse** — your photo is live and public, with the full-resolution original locked behind payment.

✅ You just onboarded as a creator and listed a pay-gated work — with nothing but an email.

## Option B — Ask the AI and watch creators get paid (Citations)

1. Go to **https://tollgate.gudman.xyz**
2. In **Ask the AI**, ask a real question the sources might cover.
3. You'll get an answer with **cited sources** — and each citation triggers a small **USDC payment to that source's creator**, on Arc testnet.
4. Open **/proof** to see the tamper-evident ledger: every payment, hash-chained, verifiable.

✅ You just saw an AI agent pay creators per citation, live on-chain.
